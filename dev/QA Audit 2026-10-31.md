## A. Executive summary

The game is in good health at its current size of 5 players.
- The validator passes with 0 errors and 0 warnings.
- All 209 tests pass.
- With 2, 5, 10, 25 and even 50 real WebSocket clients, players saw each other correctly. Nothing leaked between
  players' inventories, quests or progress.
- Shop arbitrage across all 38 shops is closed. Quest rewards can't be repeated. Chests are once per player. Range and
  line-of-sight checks held against forged messages.

The problems found fall into five groups:

1. **The main quest has a blocker (QA-01).** Pick Lock never succeeds from the real client: the page sends a message
   with two `t` keys, so the server ignores every tap. Tobias's cage can't be opened, so nobody can finish *Passage
   to the Mainland*.
2. **One player can hurt everyone else.**
   - Asking for the journal ~100 times a second freezes the world (QA-02).
   - Party invites and trade requests from anywhere freeze another player's conversation until they log out
     (QA-03).
   - Two players chopping one tree leave a stump for good (QA-05).
   - Enough shop sales fill the world's queue, and then nothing respawns (QA-06).
3. **A crash between saves can duplicate items** (QA-04). Characters and the world are saved at different moments,
   so a killed phone host, a power cut or an out-of-memory kill after a trade or a market listing leaves the item in
   two places.
4. **The Isle is a wall for new players.**
   - A level-5 Skeleton walks the graveyard where every character starts. It kills anyone who reads the graves
     (QA-10).
   - The hobgoblins' lair kills about 3 players in 4 who arrive with the Isle's kit (QA-11).
   - Frank's training advice points at targets that are locked away or deadly (QA-27).
5. **The accounts and network side has real holes** for public play:
   - a central ticket can be relayed to log in elsewhere as someone else (QA-07);
   - LAN answers can run script on the start screen (QA-08);
   - admin rights go to whoever registers an unregistered admin name (QA-09).

**Performance.** 50 players in one process just fit the 20 Hz tick on a local SSD: about 30–34 ms mean against a
50 ms budget. Three cheap fixes would roughly halve that: no file checks in the tick (QA-13), caching map lookups at
login (QA-14), and building each player's state once per tick (QA-28). Run from the Google Drive folder, the game
fails at 25 or more players because of per-tick file checks. A phone host realistically carries 5–10 players.

**In total:** 48 distinct issues after removing duplicates (14 issues were found by two or more testers).
- 2 Critical
- 13 High
- 15 Medium
- 18 Low or design notes

## C. Critical and high-priority bugs

### QA-01 · Locks / main quest · **Critical** · Reproduced
*(Security SEC-10, gameplay GP-07)*
- **Description:** Pick Lock never succeeds from the real client.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open any chest's Pick Lock, or Tobias's cage.
  2. Tap. Nothing happens: 0 pins set, 0 slips.
  - Scripts: `security\e01b_lock.py`, `security\js_dupkey.py`.
- **Expected:** taps set pins.
- **Actual:** the server drops every tap. *Passage to the Mainland* can't be finished.
- **Cause:** `web/js/games.js:79` sends `{t:"game", do:"set", t:ms}`. The second `t` wins, so the message arrives as
  `{"t":1234,...}` and is ignored.
- **Fix:** rename the time field.
- **Also, once fixed:** the server trusts the client's tap time and sends each pin's period and phase. A scripted
  client then opened 14 of 14 locks at Stealth 1. Judge taps by server time with a small latency window
  (`minigames.py:106`).

### QA-02 · Server robustness / DoS · **Critical** (public server) · Reproduced
*(Code review CR-03)*
- **Description:** one connection can freeze the world by asking for its journal over and over.
- **Steps:** send about 100 `journal` requests a second (`codereview\repro_slow_client.py`).
- **Expected:** 20 ticks a second for everyone.
- **Actual:** 13 ticks in 139 s.
- **Cause:** each journal request costs about 50 ms and about 50 KB on the server's single thread. The only limit is
  120 messages a second per connection, for every kind of message (`server/game.py:347-354`,
  `dsengine/player_actions.py:794`).
- **Fix:** separate limits for expensive requests (journal, market, rack filter, admin lists), and cache
  `journal_data` until something in it changes.

### QA-03 · Multiplayer griefing · **High** · Reproduced (real clients)
*(Security SEC-08, multiplayer MP-02, code review)*
- **Description:** any player, on any map, can replace another player's open window with a party invite, trade
  request or gift. A conversation underneath stays stuck until that player logs out.
- **Steps:**
  1. B talks to the Arena Master.
  2. A, on another map, sends `party_invite` for B.
  3. B declines.
  4. Every Talk now says "You cannot do that right now!".
  5. 100 invites in a burst send B 199 boxes.
  - Script: `multiplayer\scripts\mp_grief.py`.
- **Expected:** the request waits, or is refused because B is busy, and B's script carries on.
- **Actual:** `script_state` stays CONVERSATION for good. Shop, bank and chest windows are wiped too.
- **Cause:**
  - `homes.py:403-406`: `show_menu` uses `hide_action_box`, which never sets `script_result`.
  - Nothing checks range (`together.py:133`, `player_actions.py:742`).
  - Nothing throttles invites, trade requests or gifts (`together.py:366`, `511`).
- **Fix:**
  - Refuse ("X is busy") or queue requests while the target has a box open or a script running.
  - When a box must close, use `close_action_box(-1)`.
  - Require the players to be near each other, and limit requests per target.

### QA-04 · Saving / duplication · **High** · Reproduced (hard-killed server)
*(Multiplayer MP-03, code review CR-04)*
- **Description:** a crash between saves duplicates or loses items.
- **Steps:**
  - **Trade** (`multiplayer\scripts\mp_trade.py 1`, kill the server, then `mp_trade.py 2`):
    1. A (saved) trades 500 gold to B.
    2. B logs out (saved).
    3. The server is killed.
    4. On restart A has 500 and B has 500.
  - **Market** (`codereview\repro_torn_save.py`):
    1. A lists 5 logs.
    2. Another player leaves, which saves the world.
    3. The server crashes.
    4. The logs are in both the pack and the market.
- **Expected:** the total is unchanged.
- **Actual:** the items are doubled. The owner of a host can repeat this at will.
- **Cause:** characters are saved one at a time (on join, quit, leave, the 60 s autosave). `save_all` saves
  characters and the world in separate transactions. The world is also saved whenever someone leaves.
  (`server/game.py:124-137, 166-176, 741-743, 784-793`; `store.py:96-105`.)
- **Fix:** whenever something moves between characters, or between a character and the world (trade, gift, market,
  town project), save both sides in one transaction. Better still, a "dirty" save per tick in one transaction.

### QA-05 · Gathering / shared world · **High** · Reproduced
*(Multiplayer MP-01)*
- **Description:** two players chopping one tree or mining one rock leaves it a stump or bare rock until the server
  restarts, and both get the yield.
- **Steps:** two players Chop Tree002 at 1,1,0 (19,19) within 0.5 s. After 200 s it's still `Tree_Stump001`. Rocks
  do the same. Script: `multiplayer\scripts\mp_tree.py`.
- **Expected:** it grows back after 20 s.
- **Actual:** it never does, and the yield is doubled.
- **Cause:** `scripting.py:836-841`. `script_change_tile` records the current tile as the one to put back. The second
  change records the stump, and its restore runs last.
- **Fix:** skip the change and its restore when the cell is already the target tile. Or restore only if the cell
  still holds what this change put there.

### QA-06 · World queue · **High** · Reproduced
*(Code review CR-02)*
- **Description:** once the action queue is full, nothing respawns and dropped items never despawn, for everyone.
- **Steps:** sell Bread one at a time 3000 times, then kill an NPC and drop a log. Neither is queued. Script:
  `codereview\repro_queue_full.py`.
- **Cause:** the queue holds at most 3000 entries and quietly drops new ones (`world.py:1233`). Every shop purchase
  or sale adds an entry that lasts 1–4 hours (`player_items.py:460, 492`).
- **Fix:**
  - Merge shop restocks per rack and item, or keep shop stock outside the queue.
  - Never drop respawns.
  - Log loudly when the queue is nearly full.

### QA-07 · Accounts / central server · **High** · Reproduced
*(Security SEC-01)*
- **Description:** a malicious game server can take a player's central ticket and log in on another server as
  them.
- **Steps:**
  1. Server M answers `/api/info` with server V's id.
  2. The start screen gets a ticket made out to V and sends it to M.
  3. M sends it to V and gets "welcome as Victim".
  - Script: `security\t02_ticket_relay.py`.
- **Cause:** the ticket is bound only to the id the server reports about itself (`web/home/index.html:457-463`,
  `central/store.py use_ticket`, `server/game.py:411-438`).
- **Fix:**
  - Central returns its own stored URL for that id, and the page goes only there.
  - No tickets for unregistered addresses or LAN servers, or have the target prove it holds its central secret by
    signing a nonce.

### QA-08 · Start screen / XSS · **High** · Reproduced (Playwright)
*(Security SEC-02)*
- **Description:** a LAN answer's `online` and `max` fields are put into `innerHTML`, so anyone on the same network
  can run script on the start screen. That script has the central token and `window.DSApp`.
- **Steps:** `security\t09_lan_xss.py`.
- **Cause:** `web/home/index.html:489-491`. The apps pass LAN answers on unfiltered (`desktop/app.py find()`,
  `Net.java:70-77`).
- **Fix:** use `Number()` or `textContent` for these fields, and keep only typed, known fields in the apps.

### QA-09 · Admin rights · **High** (depends on config) · Reproduced
*(Security SEC-03)*
- **Description:** an admin name in `DS_ADMINS` or `DS_CENTRAL_ADMINS` that nobody has registered yet can be
  registered by anyone, and that account is an admin.
- **Steps:**
  - With `DS_ADMINS=Kris`, register `kris` and get admin.
  - On central, the same gives the admin API: every account's email, approving servers, bans, ads.
  - Scripts: `security\t01_admin_squat.py`, `security\t03_central_admin_and_limits.py`.
- **Cause:** `server/game.py:559`, `central/app.py is_admin`.
- **Fix:** grant admin only by the account flag, or make the env admin names unregistrable (also for a first-time
  ticket account).

### QA-10 · New-player experience · **High** · Reproduced

- **Actual:** no Isle build wins: 0 of 40 duels at Strength/Defence 2 with wooden gear, 2 of 40 at Strength/Defence 3

- **Cause:** `maps/3,1,0.txt:26`, `NPC Skeleton 224 96 Attitude=Bad`.
- **Fix (your call):** remove it, move it to the crypt, have it come out only at night, or replace it with a
  level-1 creature.

### QA-12 · View sync · **High** · Reproduced
*(Code review CR-01)*
- **Description:** other players keep seeing a stance, invisibility, sleep or a cart ride after it ends.
- **Steps:** Bea goes Sneaking and invisible, then both end. The only change sent is `[{'u': 2431}]`, so other pages
  keep `sc='Sneaking'` and `gh=1`. Script: `codereview\repro_stale_keys.py`.
- **Cause:** the snapshot diff never says when a key has gone (`view.py:149-156`), and the page merges with
  `Object.assign` (`state.js:101`). Affected keys:
  - on players: `sc`, `gh`, `zz`, `rd`, `pvp`;
  - on NPCs: `dn`, `lk`, `cone`.
- **Fix:** list removed keys in the diff (null, or an `rm` list), and have the page delete them.

### QA-13 · Performance · **High** (Critical when run from Google Drive) · Measured
*(Performance PERF-01)*
- **Description:** every tick, for every chest in each player's view, the server checks script files on disk
  (`os.path.getsize`).
- **How measured:** `performance\engine_bench.py`, `performance\profile_fresh.py`.
- **Numbers:**
  - 37–200 file checks per tick.
  - Each costs 22 µs on a local disk and 120–800 µs on G:.
  - From G:, 9.1 s of a 32 s profile.
  - 50 new characters on G: run at 107 ms a tick (9 snapshots/s) with CPU only half busy.
- **Cause:** `view.py:13-15` → `locks.py:28 chest_lock` → `locks.py:66` → `scripting.py:59 script_exists`. Also
  `world.weather_on` calls `os.path.exists` every tick.
- **Fix:** cache `chest_lock` and `chest_sturdy` per chest, and keep `script_exists` as a set refreshed at most once a
  second.

### QA-14 · Performance (logins) · **High** · Measured
*(Performance PERF-03)*
- **Description:** `places()` rescans the maps folder (1,116 files) on every `place()` call. The quest list calls it
  about 34 times, and is rebuilt on login and on every attribute change.
- **Numbers:**
  - One quest list takes 103 ms on a local disk and about 0.55 s on G:.
  - When players join together, everyone's game freezes: 0.3 s for 4 joining, 0.71 s for 15, **2.2 s for 25**.
- **Cause:** `data.py:433`, `quests.py:167`, `scripting.py:1086`.
- **Fix:** check the folder's stamp at most every few seconds, as `ini.fresh` does, or cache `place()` results.

### QA-15 · Saves / performance · **High** (long-lived characters) · Measured
*(Code review CR-06, performance PERF-04)*
- **Description:** each fog cell a character has seen is stored, forever, and never collapsed to "all". Saves run on
  the event loop, twice a minute per character (its own autosave and `save_all`).
- **Numbers:**

  | Maps explored | Save size | Time on the loop per save | `save_all`, 50 such players | Welcome message |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 100 | 919 KB | 33 ms | 2.2 s stall | 919 KB |
  | 300 | 2.8 MB | 136 ms | 7 s stall | 2.8 MB |

  Scripts: `performance\db_cost.py`, `codereview\explored_cost.py`.
- **Cause:** `session.py:527-554, 1015-1026`; `store.py` runs synchronously; `session.py:673` and `game.py:174`
  both save.
- **Fix:**
  - Store each map's cells as a bitset (about 26× smaller), with "all" once a map is fully seen.
  - Save in an executor thread.
  - Drop the duplicate autosave.
  - Send the explored set only when it changes.

## E. Multiplayer assessment

**Correctness is solid.**
- With 2, 5, 10, 25 and 50 real WebSocket clients:
  - nothing leaked between players;
  - no lost or misdirected chats or whispers;
  - no position mismatches;
  - the player cap was exact (10 joining a 5-player server got exactly 5).
- Shared kills give each fighter their own loot roll and run Kill scripts once each.
- Chests are per player, even when opened at the same moment.
- Town projects take exactly what's needed.
- Abrupt disconnects at every size were handled.

**The weak points are interactions, not sync:**
- what one player can do to another's windows (QA-03);
- two players on one resource (QA-05);
- what's saved when (QA-04);
- trade details (QA-22, QA-23, QA-37, QA-38);
- boss scaling (QA-24).

**Is 50 players per server realistic?**

| Players | Verdict |
|---|---|
| 5 (the current cap) | Comfortable everywhere. |
| 10 | Fine on a PC or server; fine on most phones. |
| 25 | Fine on a local disk, but logins freeze everyone for up to 2 s (QA-14), and from G: it breaks (QA-13). |
| 50 | Just inside the 20 Hz budget on a local SSD: 30–34 ms mean, about 45 ms p95, 66–75% of one core, no headroom. Long-lived explorers make save stalls of several seconds (QA-15). |

**To reach 50, fix in this order:**
1. QA-13 (file checks in the tick).
2. QA-14 (map lookups at login).
3. QA-28 (player state once per tick).
4. QA-15 (explored storage and off-loop saves).
5. QA-02 (per-kind request limits).
6. QA-04 (one-transaction saves).

Items 1–3 are each a few lines and should bring 50 clustered players to about 15–20 ms a tick (an estimate). A phone
host should stay capped at 5–10.

## F. Security and exploit assessment

**What held:**
- Path traversal on every static route.
- The 64 KB message cap.
- Range and line-of-sight checks on clicks and spells.
- Forged prices and quantities.
- Quest rewards once only.
- Chests once per player.
- No buy-and-sell arbitrage.
- Forged trade amounts (negative, infinite).
- Spell cast gaps.
- Non-admin access to admin messages.
- scrypt password hashes and 80-bit recovery codes.
- Ticket expiry and single use.

**Exploits that work today, in priority order:**

| Area | Issues |
|---|---|
| Account security | Ticket relay (QA-07); admin name squatting (QA-09); LAN script injection on the start screen (QA-08); proxy header trust (QA-19); no Origin check (QA-20); closed registration bypassed by a ticket (QA-21). |
| Denial of service and griefing | Journal flood (QA-02); window hijack and stuck scripts (QA-03); queue filling (QA-06); chat and log floods (QA-35); limits beaten by concurrent requests (QA-18). |
| Duplication | Crash after a trade or market listing (QA-04); double yield and a permanent stump (QA-05). |

| Client trust | Once QA-01 is fixed, lock picking trusts the client's tap time. That must be fixed together with it. |

For a LAN game among friends, only QA-01, QA-03 and QA-05 matter much. For a **public cloud server** with the central

## G. Performance assessment

Measured with the real server, bots walking and half of them fighting, on a local copy of the game unless marked.

| Players | Tick ms mean, spread out | Tick ms mean, clustered | Tick ms, new characters on G: | CPU % of one core, spread / clustered | RSS MB | KB/s per client, spread / clustered |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.4 | 3.3 | – | 4 / 7 | 164 | 7.6 / 11.3 |
| 5 | 4.8 | 4.7 | 13.8 | 16 / 14 | 167 | 10.0 / 12.4 |
| 10 | 7.8 | 6.9 | 30.9 | 21 / 27 | 170 | 9.6 / 16.7 |
| 25 | 17.8 | 14.0 | 62.3 | 43 / 36 | 177 | 10.1 / 20.6 |
| 50 | 34.0 | 30.3 | **107** (9 snapshots/s) | 75 / 66 | 182 | 11.7 / 25.5 |

**Server side:**
- **File checks:** per-tick file-system calls (QA-13) are the worst bottleneck on slow storage. That includes the
  Google Drive folder you run from, Android, and network shares.
- **Snapshots:** building views costs as much as the world tick itself. The O(P²) player state (QA-28) and per-viewer
  entity state (QA-29) are the limits when players gather.
- **Event-loop stalls:**
  - logins (QA-14);
  - saves (QA-15);
  - expensive requests (QA-02);
  - scrypt checks under a flood (QA-18).

  Everything runs on one asyncio thread, so any stall freezes every player.
- **Memory is fine:** 164 MB with 1 player, 182 MB with 50. Caches are capped by the map count. In a 10-minute churn
  soak, no sessions leaked and the entity count stayed flat.
- **Database:** new characters are cheap (0.06 ms a save). Explored characters are not (QA-15). Each character is
  written twice a minute.

**Client side:** steady 60 fps on desktop (1600×900) and the phone viewport (915×412) with 25 bots nearby.
- p99 frame time 16.8 ms.
- 0 long tasks.
- Heap flat at 6–10 MB.
- No console errors.
- JavaScript takes 3–5% of a core.
- Fog drawing is negligible.

A low-end phone, 4–8× slower than the test PC, may not hold 60 fps. That wasn't tested on real hardware.

**Is the architecture enough?** One Python process is enough for this game's scale (5–25 players) once the stalls
are removed. Three structural changes would carry it to 50:
- each player isolated in the tick (QA-17);
- saves in one transaction, off the event loop (QA-04, QA-15);
- an explicit way for the view protocol to say a key was removed (QA-12).

Beyond about 60–80 players, the single-threaded snapshot building would need splitting up (for example, by area).

## H. Gameplay recommendations

These are evidence and suggestions only. No balance has been changed.

**The opening hour.** The funeral, the letter and Dinner are clear and quick (about 3 minutes); the journal and NPCs
point to each next step. After Dinner the difficulty jumps:
1. A level-5 Skeleton at the start (QA-10).
2. Training advice that points at wrong targets (QA-27).
3. About 25 minutes of goblin kills to reach Strength 2.
4. A lair that kills 3 in 4 (QA-11).

A new player's first death is likely the lair, followed by a 2-minute grave and a 100-tile walk back with the wooden
sword lost (QA-42). That's a bad moment to lose a player. A training dummy at the smithy, a fair lair and keeping
wielded gear on death would smooth it.

**Early training is barely worth it.** Strength and Defence 2 add only +2 max hp and +0.67 attack: 21 of 40 duels
won against a hobgoblin at level 2, against 22 of 40 at level 1. The Amulet's Strength XP reward comes *after* the
gate it would have helped with. Reward XP is small once scaled: Dinner's "Cooking 2" comes to 0.83 of a level.

**Economy.**
- Selling pays almost nothing (sell factor 0.25: Logs 1 gold, Leather 2), and a goblin gives about 0.75 gold.
- A Healing Draught costs 42 gold, which is about 40–55 goblin kills.
- Food barely heals: Bread 1, cooked meat 2, Fried Egg 3, against 21–25 max hp.

So gold has little use early, and the one rich loop is the wooden-sword Shard farm (QA-26). Consider:
- a higher sell factor for gathered raw goods;
- food that heals in proportion to the effort of cooking it;
- a value floor for dismantling.

**Items.** `Basic_Potion` makes the Minor Healing Draught pointless (QA-45). Fold it into the three-strength scheme,
or drop it.

**Multiplayer engagement.** Boss scaling should come back down (QA-24) so a group that splits up isn't punished. The
wear reset by trade (QA-22) would quietly become "trade with your alt to repair": close it before players find it.

**Not assessed:** the mainland's 13 chapters, side quests, the Shifting Deep, homesteads, scrolls and own spells.
These need a second pass.
