Privacy policy

{{NAME}}, run by {{OPERATOR}}. This policy covers the central server (accounts, the list of games, the start screen) and the game servers it lists.

What we keep, and why

  • Your account: the name you choose, your password (only as a one-way hash: nobody can read it back), a recovery code (also only as a hash) and, if you give one, your email address. We use these to log you in, and the email only to send you a code when you've forgotten your password.
  • A log-in token, kept in your app or browser so you needn't type your password each time. Logging out removes it.
  • Your IP address, briefly, to stop password guessing and too many accounts from one place, and in the server's logs.
  • Game servers that are listed: their name, address and how many are playing.

Your characters

Each game server is a world of its own, and your characters live on the server you made them on, and only there: what you carry, your quests, your house. The server's owner keeps them, not the central server. A server that closes takes its characters with it. Your account (your name) is the same on every server.

Servers you join may keep what you do in their world, and what you say in their chat, for as long as they run. Each is run by its owner: the central server lists only servers its admins have approved, but it doesn't run them.

Ads

The start screen shows {{ADS}}. When it's Google AdSense (in a web browser), Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites: see how Google uses data from sites that use its services, and turn off personalised ads at Google's ad settings.

The Android app's start screen shows {{APPADS}}. When it's Google AdMob, Google uses your phone's advertising ID and what it knows of the device to choose and count ads: see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the EEA and the UK the app asks first, and "Ad choices" on its start screen changes your answer; anywhere, you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads). There are never ads in a game, only on the start screen. The desktop app's ads are our own and use no cookies.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data, or share it except as this page says.
  • We don't ask your real name, age or where you live.

How long, and your say

Your account stays until you ask for it to be removed. Ask, and we'll delete it (and your email) from the central server; for characters on a game server, ask that server's owner. Write to {{CONTACT}} for that, or with any question about this policy.

Children

The game isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their data. If you think a child has made an account, write to us and we'll remove it.
