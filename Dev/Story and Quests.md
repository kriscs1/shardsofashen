# Dragon Sword: the story and its quests

A summary of the quests in the game as it stands, for planning the story and making changes. The main quest line
comes first, step by step; then the side quests, by region. The last section says where each quest lives in the
game's files.

## The world in brief

- **The Isle**: a small island off the south coast where every new character begins. It has a village, a farm, a
  chapel, a hermit and a ferry. You can't leave until you have earned a ferry ticket.
- **The Southlands**: the mainland. Southport (where the ferry lands), Ashford and King Tyron's keep, Oakhollow and
  the Wildwood, Moorcross on Greymoor, Fenwick in the Saltmarsh, Highwatch on the Redrock road, Dunerest on the
  edge of the Sunscar Desert, Frosthold under Frostpeak, Thornwick in the Elderwood, Ravensgate on the Barrowdowns.
- **The Cinderlands**: north of the Elderwood, over the Heartwood bridge. Emberwatch, the Ashwold and the Cinder
  Peaks, where the cult of the Ashen Hand works to wake a dragon.
- **The Royal Isle**: the King's castle, with the court wizard at the top of its tower.

The threat that ties it together: **the Ashen Hand cult is waking Ignaroth, a dragon sleeping under Ashpeak.**

## The main quest line

Five chapters. Each one is unlocked by finishing the one before it (and by reaching a level).

### Chapter 1: Passage to the Mainland (the Isle; the tutorial)

You wake in a cottage on the east of the Isle, with **Jimmy** the cat and no sword. The ferry is the only way off.
This chapter teaches the basics, one at a time: chopping, gathering, cooking, crafting, wielding, searching,
going underground, and casting a spell.

| # | What you do | Where / who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speak to **Maren** the ferrywoman. The first crossing needs a ticket. | The pier, south of the Isle |
| 2 | Ask her sister **Bess** the milkmaid. A goblin came up from the chapel cellar in the night and stole her ticket book. You're in no state to go after it, so she sets you right first. | The farm, north of the Isle |
| 3 | **Chop 3 logs** for Bess's range, with the axe she lends you. *(woodcutting)* | Any tree |
| 4 | **Take an egg** from the hen coop and **fry it** on her range. Bring it to her. *(gathering, cooking)* | The coop; Bess's hut |
| 5 | **Make a wooden sword and shield** at the barn's workbench and **wield both**. *(crafting, equipping)* | The barn |
| 6 | Ask **Brother Aldous** about the cellar. | The chapel |
| 7 | He has lost the cellar key. **Search the barrels and crates** in the cellar. (It's only found by someone armed.) | The chapel cellar |
| 8 | Go down through the **trapdoor** (the key lets you down) into the caves. Find the book. | The caves under the Isle |
| 9 | The book is in a **locked goblin's cage**, out of reach. Ask Aldous. He gives you a **Grasp** scroll. | The chapel |
| 10 | **Cast Grasp** at the cage: an unseen hand pulls the book out. *(magic)* | The caves |
| 11 | Take the book back to Bess. She stamps you a **ticket**. | The farm |
| 12 | Show the ticket to Maren and **sail to Southport**. | The pier |

**Reward:** passage to the mainland (after the first crossing the ferry costs 1 gold).
**Unlocks:** chapter 2, *A Place of Your Own*, *The Herbalist's Remedy*.

### Chapter 2: King's Hobgoblin Hunt

| # | What you do | Where / who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speak to **King Tyron**. | His keep, south of Ashford |
| 2 | **Slay five hobgoblins** (counted in your journal). | The old iron mine, the mine depths, the warren or the goblin camp |
| 3 | Return to the King. | The keep |

**Reward:** 100 gold, a ruby, Strength experience.
**Unlocks:** chapter 3 (at level 12), *The Dwarf's Grudge*, *The Wildwood Watch*.

### Chapter 3: The Ashen Hand (level 12)

Rumours of a cult in the Cinderlands.

| # | What you do | Where / who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cross the Heartwood bridge and speak to **the Warden**. | Emberwatch |
| 2 | Find the cult's **three burnt shrines**. | The Ashwold |
| 3 | Report to the Warden. | Emberwatch |
| 4 | **Storm the Cinder Fort** and defeat **the Ashen Priest**. He carries the **Ashen Codex**. | High in the Cinder Peaks |
| 5 | Bring the Codex to the Warden. It says the cult means to wake a dragon. | Emberwatch |

**Reward:** 300 gold, 3 super potions, Strength and Defence experience.
**Unlocks:** chapter 4 (at level 15).

### Chapter 4: The Ashen Omens (level 15)

The investigation chapter. Your choices here change the final fight.

| # | What you do | Where / who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Take the Codex's news to **the court wizard**. Ash is falling on the Southlands. | The palace tower, the Royal Isle |
| 2 | He sends you to **Morwen** the bog witch, who reads omens. | Fenwick, in the Saltmarsh |
| 3 | Morwen needs a **frost lily** from the frozen lake, where the Frost Troll lives. She brews **Dragonsbane** from it. | The frozen lake, east of Frosthold |
| 4 | Find **the Ashen Hand acolyte** and learn what the cult knows of the dragon. **A choice:** | Camped in the hills west of Frosthold |
|   | - **Threaten him**: he attacks. Kill him and his notes give up the secret (and you take the Ashen Sigil). | |
|   | - **Pay him 200 gold**: he tells you the secret. | |
|   | - **Swear the cult's oath**: he tells you the secret, *and* the cult's skeletons in the lair will take you for one of their own. | |
|   | The secret: **Ignaroth has an old wound under its left wing**, where the scales never grew back. | |
| 5 | Tell the court wizard what you learned. | The Royal Isle |

**No reward of its own:** this chapter's rewards are its effects on the finale.

### Chapter 5: The Dragon of Ashpeak (level 18; the finale)

| # | What you do | Where / who |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ask the court wizard to **open a portal** to Ignaroth's lair. Bring your best armour, food and potions. | The Royal Isle |
| 2 | **Slay Ignaroth.** What chapter 4 did, as you enter the lair: | The lair under Ashpeak |
|   | - Morwen's **Dragonsbane**: the dragon is weakened (fewer hitpoints). | |
|   | - **The old wound**: its defence is lower. | |
|   | - **The oath** (if you swore it): the two skeletons guarding the lair let you pass. | |
| 3 | Step into the portal and tell the wizard. | The Royal Isle |

**Reward, a choice:** the **Dragon Sword** *or* Ignaroth's hoard (**2500 gold**), plus Strength and Defence
experience. The wizard remarks on it if you swore the cult's oath.
**Unlocks:** the gates of **Wardhaven**, over the bridge east of Ashford's farms.

## Side quests

### On the Isle

**The Hermit's Fire**: *Woodcutting 10*
The hermit in the Isle's western woods wants 5 oak logs. Reward: 25 gold, an oak staff, Woodcutting experience.

### Ashford and King Tyron's lands

**A Place of Your Own**: *after chapter 1*
The settler by Ashford's homestone wants 10 planks and 4 stone blocks, made at her carpenter's bench (planks from
logs, blocks from stone mined with a pickaxe). Reward: the **Access Crystal**, your own homestead to build on, and
Construction experience.

**The Herbalist's Remedy**: *level 3, after chapter 1*
The Herbalist in her cottage on the Ashford farmlands wants 3 cave mushrooms (the old iron mine) and 2 spider silk
(giant spiders in the mine depths). Reward: 60 gold, 2 super potions, Herblore experience.

**The Beggar's Lost Key**: *level 3*
Give the beggar in Ashford a coin and he tells you how the hobgoblins chased him out of the mine and took his key
down to their warren. Find it and unlock his old brick house on the Isle: it's yours. (He warns of giant rats in
its cellar.) Reward: the house.

**The Smugglers' Run**
Sergeant Holt at the castle's south gate wants to know how smuggled goods reach the market. The fishermen mention
an old barrel on the lake path that nobody moves. **Search it**: it hides a tunnel to a smugglers' camp on the
cove. **Sneak to their leader, Red Meg, unseen.** The smugglers only see ahead of them, never through cargo,
tents or walls. Meg offers a deal (her trade stays off Holt's streets if his men stay off her cove). Tell Holt.
Reward: 60 gold; the smugglers become friendly, and Meg's shop is open to you.

**The Dwarf's Grudge**: *level 8, after chapter 2*
Brokk the dwarf, in the Deep Hollow (through the hatch in the hobgoblin warren), wants 4 skeletons smashed and the
cave troll slain. Reward: 150 gold, 2 sapphires, a steel sword, Mining experience.

### The Wildwood and Oakhollow

**The Wildwood Watch**: *level 5, after chapter 2*
Ranger Ash in Oakhollow sends you to visit the three old watch stones (the north road, the western woods, the
river), then to clear the spider nest west of Oakhollow (5 giant spiders). Reward: 120 gold, a yew staff,
Strength and Woodcutting experience.

**Old Hob**: *after Bog Lights*
Old Hob of Oakhollow is missing and his house is boarded up, but a shutter hangs loose. His diary says a giant
spider bit him and he feels himself going wild. Ask Morwen for a cure, find the wild man in the Wildwood's western
woods, and use her calming draught on him. **A choice** about his chest (150 gold, which you may have already
emptied):
- He offers his savings to you; or
- if you took them, **give them back** (he rewards you 40 gold, keeps you as a friend) **or keep them** (the
  madness takes him again and he attacks, for good).

### Greymoor, the Saltmarsh and the Redrock road

**Wolves of the Moor**: *level 6*
The shepherd in Moorcross: slay 6 moor wolves, then find the wolf den on the eastern moor and kill the Alpha Wolf.
Reward: 150 gold, a steel shield, Defence experience.

**Bog Lights**: *level 6, Herblore 5*
Old Morwen the witch in Fenwick wants 4 bog myrtle. She brews something for Elder Pike; take it to him, then tell
her he drank it. Reward: 80 gold, 2 mega potions, Herblore experience. (Morwen returns in *Old Hob* and chapter 4.)

**Bandits on the Road**: *level 9*
Captain Hale in Highwatch: slay 5 bandits, then find their camp in the eastern hills and take the stolen goods
from their strongbox. Reward: 200 gold, a steel platebody, Strength experience.

### The desert, the north and the downs

**The Lost Caravan**: *level 11, after Bandits on the Road*
Qadir the merchant in Dunerest: find his caravan wreck in the desert west of town, slay the scorpions there and
bring back its ledger. Reward: 180 gold, 2 rubies, Crafting experience.

**The Frozen Forge**: *level 13*
Brann the forgemaster in Frosthold: bring 5 coal to light his forge, slay the Frost Troll that smashed his
waterwheel (on the frozen lake, where chapter 4's frost lily grows), then bring 2 mithril bars. Reward: 250 gold, a
mithril sword, Smithing experience.

**The Withering Wood**: *level 12, after Wolves of the Moor*
The Druid in Thornwick: touch the Heartwood, the Elderwood's old heart stone, then slay 4 blight-maddened bears.
Reward: 150 gold, an emerald ring, Herblore and Woodcutting experience.

**The Barrow King**: *level 15, after The Withering Wood*
The Gravekeeper in Ravensgate: find the old king's barrow on the downs and lay the Barrow King to rest. Reward:
250 gold, a mithril helm, Defence experience.

### The Cinderlands

**The Lost Patrol**: *level 14*
The Scout in Emberwatch: her patrol never came back from the Ashwold. Recover 3 Scout's Tags from the ember hounds.
Reward: 200 gold, a yew bow and 100 arrows.

## How the quests lead into one another

```
Passage to the Mainland (1)
├── King's Hobgoblin Hunt (2)
│   ├── The Ashen Hand (3) ── The Ashen Omens (4) ── The Dragon of Ashpeak (5) ── Wardhaven opens
│   ├── The Dwarf's Grudge
│   └── The Wildwood Watch
├── A Place of Your Own
└── The Herbalist's Remedy

Wolves of the Moor ── The Withering Wood ── The Barrow King
Bandits on the Road ── The Lost Caravan
Bog Lights ── Old Hob

Free-standing: The Beggar's Lost Key, The Hermit's Fire, The Frozen Forge, The Lost Patrol, The Smugglers' Run
```

Recurring characters worth keeping consistent: **Morwen** the witch (Bog Lights, Old Hob, chapter 4), **the court
wizard** (chapters 4 and 5; he also gives every new visitor some scrolls), and **the Frost Troll's lake** (The
Frozen Forge and chapter 4).

## Loose threads and hooks

- **Wardhaven** opens after the dragon, but nothing happens there yet: the natural place for what comes next.
- **Swearing the Ashen Hand's oath** is remembered, but only the wizard and the lair's skeletons react. The cult,
  the Warden or the King could.
- A villager in the Isle village says her granny warned never to dig in the **stone circle** on the north-west
  headland. Nothing is buried there yet (its hidden chest is opened, not dug up).
- **Bess's ticket book** was stolen by a goblin from the caves under the Isle: the goblins there are never
  explained.
- The beggar's house has **giant rats in its cellar** and a fallen-in back wall: room for a small Isle quest.

## For editors: where each quest lives

Every quest is a section of `Data/Quests.ini`: its giver, its stages (the journal text for each), its markers on the
map, its type and chapter, and what it requires. Its progress is a number kept per player (the `Attribute`), set
by the scripts in `Data/Scripts/` (named for what you do: `Talk to Milkmaid.txt`, `Kill Hobgoblin.txt`,
`Search Cellar_Crate.txt`...).

| Quest | Attribute | Main scripts |
|---|---|---|
| Passage to the Mainland | Passage | Talk to Woman, Talk to Milkmaid, Talk to Chapel_Verger, Search Cellar_Barrel / Cellar_Crate, Open / Grasp Goblin_Cage |
| King's Hobgoblin Hunt | HobgoblinQuest | Talk to King_Tyron, Kill Hobgoblin |
| The Ashen Hand | AshenHandQuest | Talk to Warden, Ashen Shrine 1-3, Cinder Fort Gate, Kill Ashen_Priest |
| The Ashen Omens | OmenQuest | Talk to Wizard, Talk to Witch, Talk to / Kill Ashen_Acolyte |
| The Dragon of Ashpeak | DragonQuest | Talk to Wizard, 0,0,-9 (the lair: Dragonsbane, the wound, the oath), Kill Dragon, Lair Portal |
| The Hermit's Fire | HermitQuest | Talk to Hermit |
| A Place of Your Own | HomeQuest | Talk to Homestead_Settler |
| The Herbalist's Remedy | RemedyQuest | Talk to Herbalist |
| The Beggar's Lost Key | BeggarQuest | Talk to Beggar, Unlock Door001 |
| The Smugglers' Run | SmugglerQuest | Talk to Smuggler_Sergeant, Search Suspicious_Barrel, Talk to Red_Meg |
| The Dwarf's Grudge | DwarfQuest | Talk to Dwarf, Kill Skeleton, Kill Cave_Troll |
| The Wildwood Watch | RangerQuest | Talk to Ranger, Watch Stone 1-3, Kill Giant_Spider |
| Old Hob | HobQuest | Read Hobs_Diary, Talk to Witch, Use Calming_Draught With Hob, Talk to Hob |
| Wolves of the Moor | WolfQuest | Talk to Shepherd, Kill Wolf, Wolf Den, Kill Alpha_Wolf |
| Bog Lights | BogQuest | Talk to Witch, Talk to Elder |
| Bandits on the Road | BanditQuest | Talk to Captain, Kill Bandit, Open Strongbox |
| The Lost Caravan | CaravanQuest | Talk to Merchant, Caravan Wreck, Kill Scorpion |
| The Frozen Forge | ForgeQuest | Talk to Forgemaster, Kill Frost_Troll |
| The Withering Wood | DruidQuest | Talk to Druid, Heartwood, Kill Bear |
| The Barrow King | BarrowQuest | Talk to Gravekeeper, Barrow Door, Kill Barrow_King |
| The Lost Patrol | PatrolQuest | Talk to Scout |

House rules for new writing (from `CLAUDE.md`): notices and signs never give hints; a conversation answer that
belongs to one stage of a quest shows only at that stage; each player's chest loot and quest progress are their
own.
